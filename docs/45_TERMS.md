## Purpose
Set the contractual terms between TryMerchantAI and merchants: service description, acceptable use, AI-specific terms, billing, liability, and termination. Especially important given we take actions on the merchant's store.

## 1. Agreement & eligibility
- By installing/using the app, the merchant (an authorized store representative) agrees to these Terms + the Privacy Policy (`44`). Must be legally able to contract + operate a Shopify store in good standing.

## 2. Service description
- TryMerchantAI is an AI-assisted commerce operations app: it analyzes the store, provides recommendations, and — **with the merchant's authorization** — executes actions across SEO, CRO, content, marketing, email, inventory, support, theme, and analytics via AI agents.
- The service integrates with Shopify APIs under scopes the merchant grants (`26`).

## 3. AI-specific terms (critical)
- **Nature of AI:** outputs are generated by AI + heuristics and may be **imperfect or inaccurate**. Recommendations are advisory; the merchant is responsible for reviewing actions.
- **Authorization + control:** agents act only at the autonomy level the merchant configures (trust ladder `00` P2). Above "Approve," the merchant explicitly authorizes unattended, reversible actions within guardrails, and may pause/kill-switch at any time.
- **Reversibility:** we design actions to be reversible or gated (`00` P3), but the merchant acknowledges some actions may be impractical to reverse; irreversible actions require explicit confirmation.
- **No guarantee of results:** we do not guarantee specific revenue, ranking, or performance outcomes; MAVD figures are good-faith estimates with stated confidence (`29`).
- **Merchant responsibility:** the merchant is responsible for the legality + accuracy of content/actions on their store (e.g., marketing claims, pricing), including AI-assisted ones, and for reviewing outputs.
- **Third-party AI:** processing uses third-party providers (`44`); merchant consents to such processing as described.

## 4. Acceptable use
- No unlawful use, abuse of the AI (e.g., generating deceptive/illegal content), reverse engineering, circumventing limits/billing, overloading the service, or using it to harm shoppers or third parties. No use to build a competing dataset/model from our service. We may suspend for violations.
